# Uganda Health Data Platform

An independent Greylad prototype aligned conceptually with Uganda's National Data Strategy 2026/27–2030/31. **Not an official government or clinical system.**

## Scope
- Medicine utilisation, indications and demographic groups
- Suspected adverse-event and pharmacovigilance review logs
- Disease summaries and treatment outcomes
- Care, admissions and surgeries
- Medicine supply, estimated days of stock cover, unmet demand
- Facility directories and national reporting concepts
- Exploratory analytics, data-quality checks and CSV / JSON exports
- Separate from Pharmacy OS and VybaCash

## Demo
Open `index.html` in a browser. Records are fictional and are saved in browser localStorage. This is a client-side demonstration, **not a secure clinical datastore**. Do not enter real patient or confidential facility data.

## Before real-world deployment
Institutional approvals, lawful data processing, role-based access, audit logs, encryption, verified facilities, validated medicine/diagnostic vocabularies, secure integrations, and authorised pharmacovigilance routing are required. Suspected events are not proof of causality. Risk calculations use a simple stock-cover formula for illustration.
